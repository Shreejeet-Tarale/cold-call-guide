
# Cold Call Transcript Findings

## 1. Initial Sample: T001–T015

This section contains preliminary findings from the first 15 transcripts.
These findings must be updated after reviewing all 100 calls.

### Common themes

1. **Purpose clarity:** Prospects sometimes ask who is calling, where the caller is from, or what the proposed growth report means.
2. **Contact relevance:** Some prospects are not the correct person, no longer work at the listed company, or can refer the caller elsewhere.
3. **Callback requests:** Prospects request callbacks at a specific time or after a short delay.
4. **Objections:** Prospects decline because they are not interested, do not see a current need, or are not responsible for the relevant work.
5. **Outcome logging:** The recorded outcome does not always capture the conversation accurately.

### Examples to investigate further

- T002: Callback requested after 30 minutes, but outcome is `not_logged`.
- T005: Callback requested after one hour, but outcome is `not_logged`.
- T010: Another contact is provided, but outcome is `agreed_to_receive_report`.
- T012: Prospect requests an explanation of the report before declining.
- T014: Prospect asks for an evening callback to identify the correct contact.

### Preliminary product implications

- Make the purpose of the call easy to understand.
- Provide quick options for wrong person, not interested, callback, and referral.
- Capture the prospect's words without requiring a long note.
- Record the next action and its timing.
- Require a clear final outcome when closing a call.

### Status

These are initial observations, not final conclusions.
Review the remaining transcripts before finalising the stage content
or estimating overall outcome frequencies.

## 2. Second Sample: T016–T030

### Call-by-call observations

- T016: Poor audio prevented the conversation from progressing clearly. Recorded outcome: `call_again`.
- T017: Prospect declined after hearing the service explanation. Recorded outcome: `not_interested`.
- T018: Prospect was unwilling to disclose internal information and was not the relevant line manager. Recorded      outcome: `not_interested`.
- T019: Prospect requested WhatsApp communication. Recorded outcome: `not_logged`.
- T020: Prospect politely declined the proposal. Recorded outcome: `do_not_call`.
- T021: Prospect said the work was handled internally and was not outsourced. Recorded outcome: `do_not_call`.
- T022: Prospect confirmed they were not associated with the listed company. Recorded outcome: `wrong_person`.
- T023: Prospect requested information by WhatsApp or email. Recorded outcome: `not_logged`.
- T024: Prospect directed the caller to the CFO, who handled the relevant information. Recorded outcome: `do_not_call`.
- T025: Prospect directed the caller to the company's Indian distributor. Recorded outcome: `wrong_person`.
- T026: Prospect requested a report through WhatsApp and agreed to a follow-up the next week. Recorded outcome: `next_step_agreed`.
- T027: Prospect scheduled a callback for 1:30. Recorded outcome: `follow_up`.
- T028: Communication problems left the correct person's identity unclear. Recorded outcome: `wrong_person`.
- T029: Prospect said they were not the right person and declined to provide another contact. Recorded outcome: `wrong_person`.
- T030: Prospect was not involved in business development but suggested another possible contact. Recorded outcome: `wrong_person`.

### Additional themes

1. **Information-first preference:** Some prospects prefer receiving information through WhatsApp or email before agreeing to a longer conversation.
2. **Referral opportunities:** A wrong contact may identify a more relevant person or an appropriate distributor.
3. **Confidentiality concerns:** Prospects may be unwilling to disclose company information or may not have authority to do so.
4. **Scheduled callbacks:** A useful follow-up record should capture the agreed time.
5. **Outcome accuracy:** Recorded outcomes sometimes lose important context, such as referrals, requested materials, or the distinction between declining and requesting no further calls.
6. **Communication problems:** Poor audio and unclear connections can prevent a meaningful conversation.

### Preliminary product implications

- Add quick actions for sending information, scheduling callbacks, recording referrals, and noting communication problems.
- Capture follow-up channel, agreed time, and referral details where applicable.
- Distinguish `not_interested` from `do_not_call`.
- Preserve the prospect's own words in a notes field.
- Keep the recorded outcome separate from individual stage results.

### Status

These observations cover T016–T030 only. Validate their prevalence against the remaining transcripts before finalising stage content or overall outcome statistics.

## 3. Third Sample: T031–T045

### Call-by-call observations

- T031: The number belonged to someone else, who had used it for 10–11 years and did not have the intended person's number. Recorded outcome: `call_again`.
- T032: The prospect requested Kannada, and the caller said someone else would call back. Recorded outcome: `call_again`.
- T033: The prospect was new to the company, was not the right person, and requested a callback after a customer meeting. Recorded outcome: `interested`.
- T034: The prospect repeatedly asked about the call's purpose, but the explanation remained unclear. Recorded outcome: `moved_company`; the transcript does not confirm that the prospect moved companies.
- T035: The prospect asked where the report would be published and agreed to a call the following day at 5 PM. Recorded outcome: `interested`.
- T036: The prospect was travelling, would return on Tuesday, and requested details by email. Recorded outcome: `not_logged`.
- T037: The prospect explicitly asked not to receive the report again after the caller explained it in Marathi. Recorded outcome: `not_interested`.
- T038: The prospect confirmed leaving the listed company the previous year. Recorded outcome: `moved_company`.
- T039: The prospect stated they no longer worked for the listed company. Recorded outcome: `moved_company`.
- T040: The prospect stated they no longer worked for the listed company. Recorded outcome: `moved_company`.
- T041: The prospect referred the caller to the head office and product team but could not provide a contact number. Recorded outcome: `do_not_call`.
- T042: The prospect questioned the report's value for a small company and arranged a Monday callback at 5 PM for a 15-minute discussion. The transcript ends mid-sentence. Recorded outcome: `not_logged`.
- T043: The prospect did not want new customers or expansion and saw no current need for growth. Recorded outcome: `not_interested`.
- T044: The prospect explained that the relevant decisions were made at the US corporate headquarters. Recorded outcome: `not_interested`.
- T045: The prospect explained that the listed business had been acquired and no longer existed under that name. Recorded outcome: `not_logged`.

### Additional themes

1. **Language preference:** Some conversations need a different language or language support.
2. **Specific callbacks:** Callbacks may have an agreed day, time, and duration.
3. **Outdated contact information:** Numbers, job roles, and company names may no longer be valid.
4. **Decision-making authority:** The right decision-maker may be at head office or another location.
5. **Explicit refusal:** A request not to receive further messages must be distinguished from a routine decline.
6. **Business fit:** Some prospects are satisfied with their current business and have no interest in expansion.
7. **Outcome discrepancies:** The stored outcome may not match the conversation. T034 is one example where `moved_company` is not supported by the transcript shown.

### Preliminary product implications

- Record preferred language where relevant.
- Capture callback date, time, and agreed action.
- Provide separate options for wrong number, former employee, and company acquired or renamed.
- Capture referral details and decision-making location.
- Distinguish `not_interested` from `do_not_call`.
- Preserve recorded outcomes separately from transcript-based observations.

### Status

Findings now cover T001–T045. These observations are preliminary and should be validated against the remaining transcripts before finalising stage content or overall outcome statistics.
